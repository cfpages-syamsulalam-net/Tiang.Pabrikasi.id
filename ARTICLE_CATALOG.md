# Article Catalog — tiang.pabrikasi.id

Scope: 96 Indonesian editorial briefs that turn pole-system requirements into auditable design, fabrication, protection, installation, maintenance, and procurement decisions. Every topic expands into six distinct reader jobs; none is a city/province/region substitution.

Domain boundaries: existing product and installation routes own transactional intent. These briefs provide neutral education and commercial qualification, not project-specific structural, geotechnical, welding, coating, electrical, lightning-protection, lifting, or K3 approval. Other owned domains may independently address the same entities from different viewpoints; only overlap inside `tiang.pabrikasi.id` is treated as cannibalization.

Catalog statistics: **16 topics · 96 briefs · 6 briefs per topic · 12 Wave-1 assets**.

Priority legend: **P0** foundational/risk-critical; **P1** important decision support; **P2** specialist depth; **P3** enrichment. Waves are bounded production groups, not a requirement to publish everything.
